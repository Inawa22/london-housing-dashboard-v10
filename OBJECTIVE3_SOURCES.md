# Objective 3 — sources, extraction and additional datasets

## Implemented from the five user-provided Google Drive workbooks

1. **Statutory Homelessness** (MHCLG), four 2025 quarters, local authority detailed tables (`A1`, `TA1`, `A2P`, `A2R`).
   - Original files retained in `data/sources/` and normalized extracts generated by `scripts/extract_homelessness.py`.
   - `data/statutory_homelessness_2025.csv` — assessed households, owed duty, prevention/relief, period-end temporary accommodation households and children, rates, types.
   - `data/homelessness_reasons_2025.csv` — top-level homelessness reasons by duty type.
   - Official source https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/live-tables-on-homelessness
   - IMPORTANT: These source copies are the user's 2025 files. GOV.UK published revised 2025 Q3/Q4 releases and a 2026 Q1 release as of 13 August 2026. The app **does not** mislabel the supplied source copies as the latest official release.
2. **CHAIN** (GLA/Homeless Link), quarter series 2024–25 Q2 through 2026–27 Q1, `P1`.
   - `data/chain_rough_sleeping.csv` — people seen rough sleeping by borough or London as published, not households; no sum of borough counts to derive unique people.
   - Official source https://data.london.gov.uk/dataset/rough-sleeping-in-london-chain-reports-2n88x
3. **London-wide overcrowding context**, GLA *Housing in London 2025* figure 4.9 (English Housing Survey).
   - `data/london_overcrowding_ehs_gla.csv` — regional rates by tenure (1994–2023 start-year records). Original workbook retained in `data/sources/`.
   - The three-year rolling survey indicator is London-wide only. It **is not** borough-level overcrowding data.

## Verified official additional sources identified, but raw downloads unavailable in this environment

- **MHCLG Table 600**: households on local-authority housing registers (waiting lists), district level. Official source/download entry: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/live-tables-on-rents-lettings-and-tenancies . As of 25 June 2026, the page includes 2024–25 Table 600. Raw download: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a2be263e50716856ed4afc4/Live_Table_600.ods . Borough waiting-list figures are **not** embedded in the app because the raw file could not be retrieved here. Waiting-list eligibility and review practices differ between boroughs; people can appear on more than one list.
- **ONS Census 2021 TS052**: occupancy rating (bedrooms), local authorities; overcrowded households have -1 or fewer bedrooms relative to bedroom standard. Latest page: https://www.ons.gov.uk/datasets/TS052/editions/2021/versions/5 . Raw CSV/XLSX cannot be fetched in this runtime; no borough overcrowding results are asserted in the app.
- **ONS RM009**: concealed families by family type and other factors, local authorities: https://www.ons.gov.uk/datasets/RM009/editions/2021/versions/4 . A concealed family does not necessarily demand another home.
- **CORE social lettings**: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/social-housing-lettings-in-england-april-2024-to-march-2025 . Annual social lettings are a flow, while waiting-list totals are a snapshot. Do not subtract raw lettings from waiting lists without modelling.

### Optional curated CSV formats

After downloading and independently validating official source files, add these files to `data/`. The Streamlit dashboard will load them automatically:

- `borough_waiting_lists.csv`: `code,year,waiting_households` with one row per borough-year, valid GSS codes `E09000001`–`E09000033` and no invented zeroes for missing data.
- `borough_overcrowding_census2021.csv`: `code,overcrowded_households,total_households` with one row per London authority. For TS052, overcrowded is the sum of -1 and -2-or-less bedroom rating categories, not all people in a household. Specify denominator transparently.

Both optional measures appear as **pending** until valid additional data are supplied. They are **not** substituted with made-up values or regional averages.

## Methodological safeguards

- Counts of households, counts of people, rates, flows and point-in-time stocks are distinct.
- Missing/suppressed source entries (e.g. `..`) are carried as nulls, not treated as zero.
- Published London statutory homelessness figures should be used as given; because some borough returns are missing/adjusted, their borough sum may not reconcile.
- CHAIN London value is a published regional count of people seen; do not sum borough counts to infer unique individuals.
- Rates of households in temporary accommodation are from Table TA1; denominators represent household projections, not dwelling stock.
- Do not add these overlapping need measures together to claim a total housing shortage.

### Optional downloader on a network-connected computer

From the project directory run `python scripts/download_wider_need_sources.py` to retrieve original MHCLG Table 600 ODS and ONS TS052 CSV, where public services permit it.
